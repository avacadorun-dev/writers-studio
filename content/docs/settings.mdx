Settings adapt the Studio to your way of working. Change one preference at a time so its effect is easy to assess and reverse.

## Interface language

Use the language selector to choose Russian, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, or French. Your choice is saved in this browser and also applies to this guide and documentation search. Changing the interface language leaves your manuscripts, project names, and dictation language unchanged. Example screenshots and the downloadable demo show their original Russian content.

## Appearance and editor

Choose a light or dark theme, a comfortable font, text size, line width, and other available editor settings. Appearance changes affect how the manuscript looks on your screen; the text is preserved.

Open a long document afterward, read a few paragraphs, and try both a normal and a narrow screen. Restore the previous choice or defaults if reading becomes harder.

## Assistant and AI providers

The server owner configures the system AI provider. Add a personal provider if you need your own plan, model, or data processing terms. A provider is the service that handles assistant or speech recognition requests.

Enter a key in its dedicated settings field. The Studio hides its value after saving. Keep it out of manuscripts, chat, screenshots, and support messages. Test the connection with the built-in button before relying on the service for important work.

## External MCP tools

MCP gives the assistant access to a separately connected tool, such as a notes database. Connect a server you trust, read its capabilities carefully, and grant the access required for your task.

Treat an MCP token like a password. Keep it private and out of documentation. To disconnect a tool, remove the connection in settings and revoke its token at the service if needed. Check afterward that the tool is no longer offered to the assistant.

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep secrets out of text">
  Support staff and the assistant do not need your full API key or password. A service name and error message with secret values removed are enough for diagnosis.
</Callout>
