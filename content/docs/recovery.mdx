---
title: Interrupted recordings
description: Find locally saved data and safely retry audio processing.
---

A lost connection or accidentally closed tab does not always mean a lost recording. The Studio saves intermediate data in the browser and shows recovered materials in the recovery center.

## Keep the tab and local data

If the page is still open, keep browser data intact and avoid starting a new recording over the old one. Check the connection, wait a few seconds, and look for a message that uploading has resumed.

Open the **Recovery center** from the projects menu. Find the session by its time, duration, and project name. Listen to the beginning or an available passage to distinguish nearby recordings, then choose recovery.

## What happens next

Saved chunks are assembled again and sent for recognition if necessary. Compare the transcript with the audio before transferring text into the manuscript. Re-transcription does not automatically replace an edited document.

If the first recognition attempt has many errors, reprocess the source audio. The previous transcript remains a reference until you select the new one.

## If nothing is found

- Use the same browser and device as the recording. Local chunks do not transfer between them automatically.
- Keep the cache and site data until recovery is complete.
- Check that you signed into the same account and opened the correct project.
- If you saved the audio as a separate file, start a new transcription from it and keep the original.

After recovery, open the document, check the inserted text, and wait for save confirmation. Remove an unwanted recovered duplicate after making that comparison.
